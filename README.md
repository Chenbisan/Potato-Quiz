# 🥔 Potato Quiz

A team quiz game that runs entirely in the browser — no install, works on Windows, Mac and phones.

**Play:** https://chenbisan.github.io/Potato-Quiz/

- 6,500+ built-in questions across 151 categories in 27 families (Pokémon, Video Games, Disney, Generations, world cultures…) and four difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard, Obscure)
- Pick by difficulty or category, 🎲 Random (bonus point) or 🎰 Gamble (double or nothing)
- 20+ streak items (Blue Shell, Exodia, Heist, Side Bet, Dodge the Bullet…), answer timer, sound effects
- Edit questions, tiers and categories; add pictures or audio clips; export/import backups from Settings

Your questions and settings are saved in your own browser on this site.

See [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) for what changed in each version.
