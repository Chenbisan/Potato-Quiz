# What's new in Potato Quiz

## 4.2.2 — 2026-10-18
- The game start sound is much gentler. It used a buzzy, harsh tone that could hurt your ears. It's the same little fanfare, now smoother and a bit quieter.

## 4.2.1 — 2026-10-18
- **🔊 Volume in the ☰ menu:** change the overall volume mid-game without leaving the game. It's the same setting as Settings › Audio › Master.
- Sound effects are a bit softer by default (70%). If you hadn't changed the effects volume, yours is lowered to match. If you had, it's left as you set it.

## 4.2.0 — 2026-10-18
- **🚩 Flag a question:** if a question looks wrong, confusing, too easy, too hard or outdated, flag it. The host can flag it under the answer or from the ☰ menu ("Flag last question"), and phone players can flag it from their phone, even after the game moves on. You can add a short note. Flags are sent in for review, and they're saved and sent later if you're offline.
- **📤 Export analytics** (on the Stats screen): download a small file of how often questions, categories and items come up, to help balance the game. It never includes player names.
- **Music now plays from audio files** that loop seamlessly. It's the same two tracks (🛗 Lounge and 🎤 Game show), but the host screen no longer has to build the music note by note while you play, so it's lighter on the computer running the game.

## 4.1.2 — 2026-10-18
- **Fairer question mix:** every family you have turned on now gets an even share of questions, and so does every category inside a family. Big families like Television and Dentistry no longer crowd out the smaller ones. It's still random, though: any family can come up next, even the one you just had.
- The Join a game screen now has a **← Menu** button, like the other screens, so you can get back to the main menu if you opened it by mistake or can't find the game.
- **Louder sound:** music and sound effects were much too quiet, even at full volume. They're now about 5× louder, at the level of a normal app or video, and still evenly matched, with the music sitting under the effects. Lots of sounds at once no longer crackle.
- Fixed: the How to Play "Got it!" button was hidden below the bottom of the box, so it looked like there was no way out. It's now always visible at the bottom of tall pop-ups, and Esc closes How to Play too.
- The main menu now fits on one screen without scrolling, on any size of screen.
- **Question stats:** new Top 500 and Top 1000 options, plus filters for family, category and difficulty.

## 4.1.1 — 2026-10-18
- Fixed: in phone games, when your bag was full the "drop an item" choice went to the next player's phone, so they could drop your items and your own choice didn't count. Now it goes to your phone, and everyone else sees that you're choosing.

## 4.1.0 — 2026-10-18
- **🎵 Background music:** a mellow 🛗 Lounge (elevator jazz) or upbeat 🎤 Game show loop plays on the host screen while you play. It's made by the game itself, so there's nothing to download, and it never plays on players' phones.
- Mute it anytime with the 🎵 button in the corner, or from the ☰ menu during a game.
- **🔊 New Audio settings:** master, music and sound-effect volumes, music style, and the sound switches (moved here from Effects). Sound effects are now leveled so none is much louder than the rest, and the music always sits a bit under them.
- The player whose turn it is now pulses gently in the top bar.
- When a phone player answers, the host's Next button drains from top to bottom as the countdown runs.

## 4.0.4 — 2026-10-18
- **📊 New player stats:** a highlights row (most wins, sharpest player, questions played, Exodia record) and a card for each player in their color, with win rate and accuracy bars, right and wrong answers, and their best and worst categories. Sort by wins, win rate, accuracy or games.

## 4.0.3 — 2026-10-18
- Fixed: opening the site again in a tab you'd hosted in before jumped straight to the setup screen with your old room. It now opens on the main menu. Reloading while hosting still reopens your room.

## 4.0.2 — 2026-10-18
- Clearer item wording, the same on the host screen and on phones: you can use 1 item per turn, and only before you pick a question.
- After a phone answer, the result now shows for 3 seconds (was 2) before the game moves on.
- Item cards now stay on screen for 6 seconds (was 10).
- Both timings are under Settings › Timer › Pacing.

## 4.0.1 — 2026-10-18
- Fixed: players from a phone game no longer show up as players added on the host screen the next time you set up a game.

## 4.0.0 — 2026-10-18
- **📱 Play on your phones:** open a room on the setup screen and everyone joins from their own phone by scanning the QR code or typing the 4-letter room code. Put the host screen on a TV (casting works great) and play from the couch.
- **Your turn, your phone:** pick a difficulty or category, use your items (including choosing who to target, Category Lock and Exodia), and tap your answer right on your phone. The result shows for a moment and the game moves on by itself. You can change how long under Settings › Game › Playing on phones.
- Everyone else's phone follows along with the question and the scoreboard.
- **Drops and refreshes are fine:** if a phone locks, loses signal or reloads, it rejoins by itself. If the host's page reloads or the tab closes mid-game, you can resume right where you left off, and phones reconnect on their own.
- Friends who arrive after the game starts can watch on their phone and join the next game.
- A phone game is all on phones: opening a room clears players added on the host screen, so names can't clash.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-17
A big update from playtesting.

**Items**
- **Fairer item drops.** Every item can now be a question prize, and rarer items really are rarer: any single Legendary shows up less often than any single Rare. An item that already appeared in a game is less likely to appear again, so you'll see more variety.
- **Comeback items come sooner:** wrong answers in a row now give an item at **2, 4 and 6** (right answers in a row still give one at 3, 5 and 7).
- **Last-place boost:** from round 11, whoever is in last place at the start of each round gets a free Common item.
- Question prizes appear more often: **35%** chance per button (was 20%).
- **Items stack:** using Skip Turn, Hard Lock, Category Lock or Beginner's Luck on someone who already has it adds more turns.
- **Category Lock on yourself:** lock yourself into a favorite category for 1 turn.
- **New Category Lock screen:** tidy cards for each family in A–Z order, or search for any category.
- **Beginner's Luck** now lasts 5 turns, and you can't miss it: the easiest difficulty glows and shows the doubled points (~~2~~ → 4).
- Nuke now rounds the average **up**.
- Settings › Items: **Tick all / Untick all** and **Reset items to default** buttons, plus new options for streak lengths and the last-place boost.

**Scoreboard**
- **Streak tracker:** each player shows their last 5 answers as ✓/✗ dots and a meter filling up toward their next item. When someone is one answer away from an item, their row glows.

**Setup**
- The category list folds away, so **Recent players** is right under the player list.
- **🔞 After Dark (18+):** a new adults-only family about sex, dating, partying and raunchy movies, TV and music. It stays hidden until you switch on **After Dark** on the setup screen.

**Sound & timer**
- New sounds when the game starts, each turn begins and each question appears, plus soft clicks on buttons (you can turn the clicks off in Settings › Effects).
- The timer starts its countdown warning at **10 seconds** (was 5). Change it in Settings › Timer.

**Questions**
- **11,000+ new questions** (now **29,499** in total), with the most added to Anime, General Knowledge, Video Games and Movies.
- **55 new categories**, such as Isekai, Mecha, Horror Games, Indie Games, Rom-Coms & Musicals, Myths & Legends, Board & Card Games, Ice Hockey, Pro Wrestling, AI & Robots and Streaming Originals.
- The 🌍 Culture button now switches only the world culture families, not Pop Culture.
