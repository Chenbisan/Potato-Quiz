# What's new in Potato Quiz

## 3.1.1 — 2026-10-17
- **Tidier scoreboard:** each player's row now stacks neatly, with the score, the last answers and streak, active effects, and items on their own lines, so nothing gets squeezed. The scoreboard is also a little wider.
- Player names are limited to 16 characters so they always fit on screen.

## 3.1.0 — 2026-10-17
- **🔞 After Dark › X-Rated:** a new, much spicier category with 119 questions on positions, toys, kinks, slang, porn trivia and the wilder side of science and history. Like the rest of After Dark, it only appears when After Dark is switched on.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-10-17
A big update from playtesting.

**Items**
- **Fairer item drops.** Every item can now be a question prize, and rarer items really are rarer: any single Legendary shows up less often than any single Rare. An item that already appeared in a game is less likely to appear again, so you'll see more variety.
- **Comeback items come sooner:** wrong answers in a row now give an item at **2, 4 and 6** (right answers in a row still give one at 3, 5 and 7).
- **Last-place boost:** from round 11, whoever is in last place at the start of each round gets a free Common item.
- Question prizes appear more often: **35%** chance per button (was 20%).
- **Items stack:** using Skip Turn, Hard Lock, Category Lock or Beginner's Luck on someone who already has it adds more turns.
- **Category Lock on yourself:** lock yourself into a favorite category for 1 turn.
- **New Category Lock screen:** tidy cards for each family in A–Z order, or search for any category.
- **Beginner's Luck** now lasts 5 turns, and you can't miss it: the easiest difficulty glows and shows the doubled points (~~2~~ → 4).
- Nuke now rounds the average **up**.
- Settings › Items: **Tick all / Untick all** and **Reset items to default** buttons, plus new options for streak lengths and the last-place boost.

**Scoreboard**
- **Streak tracker:** each player shows their last 5 answers as ✓/✗ dots and a meter filling up toward their next item. When someone is one answer away from an item, their row glows.

**Setup**
- The category list folds away, so **Recent players** is right under the player list.
- **🔞 After Dark (18+):** a new adults-only family about sex, dating, partying and raunchy movies, TV and music. It stays hidden until you switch on **After Dark** on the setup screen.

**Sound & timer**
- New sounds when the game starts, each turn begins and each question appears, plus soft clicks on buttons (you can turn the clicks off in Settings › Effects).
- The timer starts its countdown warning at **10 seconds** (was 5). Change it in Settings › Timer.

**Questions**
- **11,000+ new questions** (now **29,499** in total), with the most added to Anime, General Knowledge, Video Games and Movies.
- **55 new categories**, such as Isekai, Mecha, Horror Games, Indie Games, Rom-Coms & Musicals, Myths & Legends, Board & Card Games, Ice Hockey, Pro Wrestling, AI & Robots and Streaming Originals.
- The 🌍 Culture button now switches only the world culture families, not Pop Culture.

## 2.3.1 — 2026-10-16
- **Click an answer, then Next:** clicking a multiple-choice answer scores it right away ("🎉 Correct! +4" or "❌ Wrong") and shows a **Next** button. Clicked by mistake? "Change result" lets the host fix it.
- Games ended early with ✕ End game no longer count toward players' Games or Wins.

## 2.3.0 — 2026-10-16
- **Click an answer:** players can tap a multiple-choice answer instead of pressing Reveal.
- **Late answers:** when the timer runs out, the host can still mark an answer correct if it was called in time. Turn this off in Settings › Timer.
- **🌍 Culture on/off** button on the setup screen switches all the world culture families at once.
- **Bigger item icons** on item cards and the scoreboard.
- **Phone a Friend** no longer appears in 2-player games.

## 2.1.3 — 2026-10-16

## 2.0.3 — 2026-10-16

- Moved questions whose category gave away the answer (for example, Finding Nemo is no longer under Dentistry).
- Fixed: the box of Pokémon Black shows Reshiram, not Zekrom.

## 2.0.2 — 2026-10-16

## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-16

## 1.9.0 — 2026-10-16
- **No-choices mode:** hide all the answers. Players say their answer out loud, then the host reveals it.
- Every question now has 5 wrong answers, so 6-answer games use real options.
- **Reset all settings to default** button in Settings › Data.
- Cleaner Settings layout.
- Random bonus is back to **1 point**, so it stays below an Easy question.

## 1.8.0 — 2026-10-16
- **6-answer mode:** choose 4 answers (normal) or 6 (harder) on the setup screen.
- The question editor has 2 optional extra wrong answers for 6-answer games.
- **Question prizes:** players in the bottom half can find an item on a question they pick. Get it right to keep it.
- **Item rarity:** items are Common, Rare or Legendary, and rarer items show up less often. Change them in Settings.
- **Item stats:** a new Items tab on the Stats screen.
- Edit Questions can filter by question type (multiple choice, open answer, picture, audio).
- Settings are split into tabs (Game, Items, Timer, Effects, Data).
- Gamble cooldown now counts turns to wait (0 = no cooldown).

## 1.7.0 — 2026-10-16
- **Points doubled:** Easy 2, Medium 4, Hard 6, Obscure 10. The default win target is **60**.
- Items doubled to match: Pickpocket steals 2/4/6, Heist 6/8/10, Blue Shell −10.
- New Gamble rule (on by default): after a Gamble, get a regular question right before gambling again.
- Edit Questions opens on **Uncategorized**, so you can see questions that still need a category.
