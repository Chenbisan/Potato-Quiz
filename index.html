      ✨ Effects

      🏷️ Question preview

      The question appears automatically afterwards (the host can also click "Show now"). Set to 0 to skip the preview.

      ⏱️ Answer timer

      The clock starts when the question appears and stops when you click Reveal answer. Click the clock to pause it. If time runs out, the answer is shown and it counts as wrong.

      🎒 Items

      Players earn an item when they get several answers right in a row (🔥 hot streak) or wrong in a row (🧊 cold streak). Cold-streak items are stronger so players who are behind can catch up. A player can use one item per turn, before picking a question.

      Separate numbers with commas. With 3, 5, 7, a player earns an item on their 3rd, 5th and 7th answer in a row.
